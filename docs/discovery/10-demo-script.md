- Open `/`. Say: "Compliance teams read dense circulars, guess which policy is affected, and email

- Point at the hero finding: "This is the seeded IRDAI clause — six-hour incident reporting — mapped
  to a policy that says 72 hours. This card is labelled illustrative; the real one is generated by
  the engine."

### 0:35–1:15 — Sign in and dashboard

- Click **Open workbench** → pick **Rajeshwari, Head of Compliance**.
- On Overview: "Seven findings await sign-off across 5 circulars and 13 clauses. The queue is ordered
  by severity and deadline. Nothing here can publish itself."

### 1:15–2:15 — The finding, with receipts

- Click **Open findings** → open the finding **"All regulated data must be stored exclusively on
  servers located within India."**
- Show, in order:
  - Severity CRITICAL, citation verified, gap status.
  - The exact source clause and page/section.
  - The mapped policy `POL-CLOUD-05` and the control card.
  - The word-level redline: old region struck through, India region added.
  - The confidence breakdown and the task owner.
- Say: "Every number here is computed. The quote is re-checked as an exact substring before it can be
  cited."

### 2:15–3:00 — The human decision

- In **Record a review decision**, keep **Approve draft** and click it.
- Show the change: header badge moves to Approved, **Decision on record** shows reviewer and time,
  review history gains an entry.
- Say: "One transaction wrote the status, the immutable review event, and the audit row. The system
  never edits the policy register; approval queues a remediation email to the owner instead."
- If Gmail is connected, drain and show the inbox: `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET"
  https://policypulse-sage.vercel.app/api/cron/email`, then open the recipient's mailbox. The task
  email arrives at the owner's real address (see hosted-demo notes above).

### 3:00–3:30 — The audit trail and role boundary

### 3:30–4:00 — Isolation and the bonus flows

### 4:00 — Close

"Deterministic extraction, verified citations, a published confidence formula, and an officer

## Judge questions and answers

- **Why not an LLM?** The brief's guardrails are citation fidelity and zero hallucination. A
  deterministic engine makes every output reproducible and testable; the architecture keeps the
  agent boundaries (extract, map, gap, draft, verify) and can swap a model in behind the verifier.
- **Is this legal advice?** No. It drafts for an officer; the officer decides; the system records it.
- **What is real vs seeded?** All 13 findings are generated from the bundled corpus and registers.
  The landing card is explicitly labelled illustrative. No mock benchmarks are shown.
- **How does it scale?** Tenant-scoped queries, cursor pagination, indexed review/date filters, bounded
  page sizes, outbox email, and a limiter interface ready for Redis.
- **What is next?** Live regulator feeds, real PDF parsing, hybrid retrieval for mapping, SSO, and
  ticket integrations behind the same review/audit contract.
